# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally (confirmed 2026-10-28):

- **Client companies.** Founders and executives at growing businesses who need more qualified sales conversations (outbound) or more capacity to execute (outsourced staff). Market inferred from the English-speaking positioning as primarily US/UK/EU; not confirmed.
- **Referral partners.** Fractional CFOs, Fractional COOs, consultants, advisors and other professionals who serve growing companies and can introduce a client that needs sales or outsourcing support.

## Product Purpose

MG Revenue increases revenue for its clients in two ways: **Sales** (creating qualified opportunities through outbound) and **Outsourcing** (placing skilled, English-speaking talent from Kosovo as a team extension). Success for the site is a visitor emailing contact@mgrevenue.com, either as a client or as a partner.

## Positioning

A Kosovo-based revenue and outsourcing company. Kosovo gives access to a young, highly capable, English-speaking workforce, used to extend client teams while keeping quality and communication at the centre. One company covers both creating demand (sales) and adding capacity (outsourcing).

## Operating Context

- Sales services: cold calling, email outreach, LinkedIn outreach, appointment setting, lead generation, SDR/BDR support.
- Outsourcing roles: Sales (SDR, BDR, Account Executive), Operations (Virtual Assistant, Personal Assistant, Data Entry), Finance (Bookkeeper, Accountant).
- Process: Define (target, role, goals, success criteria), Build (the outreach or talent solution), Execute (consistently, with the client's team), Grow (improve what works, create more capacity).
- Partner flow: Introduce, We connect, Client gets support, You earn.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static single-page site (`index.html`) served on GitHub Pages at mgrevenue.com (CNAME). No build step, no form backend.
- All contact is by email: contact@mgrevenue.com (partner enquiries use the subject "MG Revenue Partnership").
- Legal entity: MG Revenue SH.P.K.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: MG Revenue. Logo: `mg-revenue-logo.png` (metallic champagne MG monogram with a rising arrow and spaced "REVENUE" wordmark).
- Colour palette is fixed by the owner and must not change: near-black ground `#080808`, warm off-white ink `#f4f3ef`, champagne gold accent `#d4a65b`, light gold `#f0c981`, dark gold `#8c652e`, muted `#a7a6a1`.
- Copy may be tightened, but every fact must be kept (services, roles, the 10% lifetime revenue share on qualifying outsourcing introductions, Kosovo, the email).

## Evidence on Hand

- The logo only. No photographs of the team, office or work.
- No client names, logos, testimonials, case studies, statistics or press. Do not invent any.
- The only figure that is a real commitment: **10% lifetime revenue share on qualifying outsourcing introductions**.
- Imagery approved by the owner: commercially-licensed open photographs of Prishtina/Kosovo, credited, and never presented as MG Revenue's own office, team or clients.

## Product Principles

1. Revenue is the point: every section ties back to more opportunities or more capacity.
2. Plain and measurable over hype: clear process, no invented proof.
3. Two doors, one company: clients and partners each find their path quickly.
4. Kosovo is an asset to be shown with pride and honesty.
