## Scope

Mode: Persuade. Surface: the single-page site at `index.html` (mgrevenue.com). Audience: client companies and referral partners, weighted equally. Action: email contact@mgrevenue.com (clients) or the partnership subject line (partners). Constraints: fixed brand palette, static HTML on GitHub Pages, no invented proof.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The offer is one poster-scale sentence with Prishtina photographed inside it where a word would be; the page refuses the dark-agency template of glowing orbs, icon cards and a centred hero.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black #080808 ground, warm ink #f4f3ef, champagne gold #d4a65b used as whole fields (hero lower half, partner chapter), dark gold #8c652e as deep ink on gold, light gold #f0c981 for hover. Zodiak hairline capitals for display, Hanken Grotesk for text. Credited Prishtina photographs in one warm monochrome treatment, square corners, flat fills, no lines, no shadows at rest.

STORY: The visitor learns that MG Revenue grows revenue two ways (outbound sales and Kosovo-based talent), sees every channel and role, follows four plain steps, sees partners earn 10% for life, and emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Black field above a gold field meeting at about 55% height. "MORE REVENUE, / BUILT IN [library photo] KOSOVO." at 9–10vw straddles the seam and changes ink as it crosses. Wordmark top-left, four links and a "Let's talk" button top-right. The supporting sentence and the action sit on the gold field.

FORM: Prishtina as a place, candidate 6 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key 393c13f7. Signature interaction: the gold field rises on load and a live partner-earnings estimator in the partner chapter. Motion grammar: colour by chapter, staggered section entrances, press travel on controls.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
